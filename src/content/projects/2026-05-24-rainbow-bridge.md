# Introduction

My friend loves Haunted Mound. One day, I was bored and saw that his birthday was coming up, so I decided I was going to remake his favorite album on cassette, Rainbow Bridge 2. The duplication of this album on it's rare format of cassette (originally only 20 copies made) led me to also remaking the first Rainbow Bridge album on cassette which before my remake had never been translated to that format.



# Process

I'm focus on Rainbow Bridge 1 as that's the more original piece, and then I'll talk about matching up the 2 to feel like they were both made in unison.

For the first album, I started by getting 2 strong references and throwing them in photoshop: Rainbow Bridge 2's physical cassette J-Card, as well as the CD art spread for Rainbow Bridge 1.

Using the CD art, I fit the tracklist, the cover, and the general elements back into the cassette format as the 2nd album officially did. Honestly this wasn't the hardest remake/reformatting. The hardest part was just just finding the right cassettes and price, which all came from duplication.com.
