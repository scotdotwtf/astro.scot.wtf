Helen is my best friend. I think the total amount of time we spent together *(before she moved away)* was maybe a year or two, but if you saw us together you would have thought we were childhood friends. Like me, she grew up on internet culture. Niche internet jokes of our generation were the baseline of a conversation, it was the fandoms and hyper specific references that really got us together. 

Outside of our lovely friendship, I started picking up on the places she lived, visited, and held close to her. One of those places was her neighborhood. I remember during the day I saw the place she would pickup her packages at; An old dingy mailbox supreme with some fluorescent tubes making the mood that much more grimy.

All of these scenes and moments led me to build this badass picture of her in my head. These are the pictures we took, after communicating my creative vision to her about her enviornment.
